# English classes

A weekly series with the two exceptions ADR-0031 describes: one occurrence
cancelled in place, one taken over by an entry of its own.

ID: english-thursdays

```
Every Thursday at three. The 3rd of September is a public holiday, so that
Thursday has no class — and it is the series itself that says so.

SERIES_ID: english-thursdays

```
The class of the 20th is at six this once. It is an entry of its own — its own
heading, its own body — and it takes the place of the occurrence it names, so
the 20th holds this and not the three o'clock class.
